:source-highlighter: highlight.js

Petr requested three copies of the current amber palette 2, followed by a
40% increase of papers 1..3 across all five dark palettes. Numbering remains
1 green, 2 amber; new slots are 3 BW phosphor, 4 red plasma, 5 cyan VFD.
These are aesthetic starting points for human visual tuning, not simulations
of phosphor spectra or the physics of plasma/VFD displays.

== Transfer policy clarification

Petr requested avoiding routine full read-backs and separate post-transfer
mirror probes. Trust a successful transfer result, restore the original profile
and RAW state, and launch the visible mirror. Failures still must be reported.
In transfer context, `reload` repeats the last clearly identified transfer;
it does not mean a firmware rebuild/reflash or device reboot. AGENTS.md records
this convention. Boot is still required to load edited palette definitions.

Petr explicitly approved deployment. Sent `7XTGVTRM.INI` to A0; VTPUT reported
SAVED (14976 record-padded bytes). The transfer workflow restores the profile
and launches the W535 Konsole mirror. No extra read-back or mirror probe was
performed, and no reboot was issued.

== Dark blue ink readability follow-up, 260924-19

Petr reports the dark palettes otherwise look good in VTPAL, but both blue
inks need more visibility, especially at minimum backlight. A simple 20%
RGB multiplication would clip normal blue's already-high channel (242), with
only about 5% headroom to 255. He approved these visual-tuning candidates:

* In palettes 1..5, `ink03`: `0,0,242` -> `40,60,255`.
* In palettes 1..5, `ink11`: `37,89,221` -> `60,110,255`.

These add red/green to lift blue readability; they are not a calibrated 20%
perceptual increase. Exact comparison confirms only these ten INI entries
changed; palette 0 and all papers/UI colors remain untouched. Prior INI:
`build/palette-backups/260924-1918-dark-blue-inks/7XTGVTRM.INI`.
Uploaded to `A0:7XTGVTRM.INI` on request; VTPUT reported SAVED (14976 padded
bytes). No extra read-back/probes or reboot. The new blue candidates await
Petr's visual comparison after reboot.
