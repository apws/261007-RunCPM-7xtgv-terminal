Mandelbrot variants in xterm256(C2)

BROT_BS: local short name for the variant shared by Bill Shen.
Original forum attachment names: mandelbrot.z, mandelbrot.hex.
BROT_PS: local short name for Phillip Stevens' optimized variant.
Original forum attachment names: mandel.asm, mandel.hex.
His optimization includes table-based multiplication.

The sources also credit Rosetta Code's Z80 Mandelbrot example and
J. B. Langston's CP/M adaptation and colorization. BS/PS identify
the discussed variants, not exclusive authorship of all their code.
Preserved source headers remain authoritative for their own credits.

See !BROT_XX.PDF: retro-comp discussion, August 2019, titled
"K80, another Z80 SBC with RC2014 expansion on 100mmX100mm board".
Thread: https://groups.google.com/g/retro-comp/c/rFNqRrUsye8

Run SUBMIT BROT_BS or SUBMIT BROT_PS in this area. The scripts use
GCLS, SYNC, VTPAD and VTSTART/VTSTOP; helper sources live in 7xtgv(C1).
BROT_PS.ASM is a vendor Z80 dialect, NOT Digital Research 8080 ASM.

MANDEL.HEX and MANDEL.Z retain distinct older archival bytes.
The redundant MANDEL.COM matched BROT_BS.COM and was archived.
