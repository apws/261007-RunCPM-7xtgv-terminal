Keyboard settings and help

AltGr remains for text; some browser/OS shortcuts may be reserved. Paste is limited to 4096 characters.

Ctrl+Shift+1…9, and Ctrl+Alt+Shift+0 for OFF. F11 remains browser fullscreen.
